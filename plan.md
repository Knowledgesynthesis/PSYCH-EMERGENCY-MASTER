## 6. Psychiatric Emergency Reasoning Framework

Teach universal emergency logic:

1. Safety first  
2. Determine if immediate threat exists  
3. Identify medical vs psychiatric cause  
4. Evaluate intoxication/withdrawal  
5. Stabilize agitation  
6. Assess decision-making capacity  
7. Craft disposition:
   - ED monitoring  
   - Psychiatric inpatient  
   - Medical inpatient  
   - Safe outpatient plan (education)

Pitfalls:
- Misclassifying delirium as psychosis  
- Missing stimulant intoxication mimicking mania  
- Inadequate suicide risk assessment  
- Misinterpreting serotonin syndrome vs NMS  
- Using improper tranquilization strategies  
- Confusing non-dangerous agitation with life-threatening behavior
